What the statuses mean

StatusMeaningWhat happens next
NEW Ingested from the feed, not yet judged Triaged on the next pipeline run
TRIAGED Judged; the routing decision is recorded but not yet applied Routed to one of the three outcomes below
DROPPED Confidently irrelevant — high confidence, low relevance Nothing. Kept for the audit trail; you can force-escalate it
DIGEST_DIRECT Grey zone: possibly relevant, but the description was clear enough to judge. Listed as a one-liner, never transcribed Appears under Maybe interesting in the next digest
AWAITING_TRANSCRIPT Queued for a transcript — either relevant, or the description was too thin to judge Published transcript, then local transcription if the podcast allows it
TRANSCRIBED Transcript stored Summarised and scored
TRANSCRIPT_FAILED No transcript could be obtained within the retry budget Summarised from the description, labelled honestly
SUMMARIZED Momentary state between scoring and classification Sorted by score into one of the two below
READY_FOR_DIGEST Scored at or above the digest threshold A full entry in the next weekly digest
SCORED_LOW Summarised but below the threshold The collapsed audit table. The summary is kept
PUBLISHED Has appeared in a digest — as a full entry or a one-liner, which is why some have no summary Nothing further
ERROR Something failed; the traceback is on the episode Open it and retry — it resumes where it failed
